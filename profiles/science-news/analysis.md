# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the scientific importance, novelty, methodology rigor, and interest of scientific news and research papers for a general informed audience.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Groundbreaking.** Major scientific breakthroughs, landmark published papers, paradigm-shifting discoveries, or major space/medical milestones.
- **7-8: High scientific value.** Substantial research findings, novel experimental results, significant discoveries, or important climate/health findings.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Incremental research progress, interesting scientific studies, or moderate scientific developments.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Minor scientific studies, preliminary unconfirmed claims, or shallow science reporting.
- **0-2: Noise.** Pseudoscience, sensationalized unbacked claims, off-topic material, or spam.

# Evaluation guidance

Consider scientific rigor, novelty, methodology, source credibility, and broader implications. Distinguish peer-reviewed findings from preliminary preprints or unverified claims.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
