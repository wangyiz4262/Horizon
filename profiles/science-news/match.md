# Science news profile

Use this profile for scientific research, scientific journal articles, space exploration, biology, physics, chemistry, medicine, environmental science, and general scientific discoveries.

Typical items include research publications (e.g., from Nature, Science), scientific discoveries, medical research breakthroughs, astronomical observations, and scientific policy or climate reports.

Do not use this profile for commercial tech updates, software releases, financial news, or pure engineering tutorials unless there is a primary focus on scientific research or fundamental science.
