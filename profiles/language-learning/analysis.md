# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether a language learning item provides clear, practical, educational, and engaging language learning value to learners or educators.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Exceptional.** Highly engaging, well-structured lessons or linguistic insights with rich vocabulary, idiom explanations, clear grammar contexts, or effective practical exercises.
- **7-8: High value.** Useful educational content, clear audio/text lesson materials, well-explained vocabulary or concepts, and practical study resources.
- **5-6: Moderately useful.** Decent educational material or language discussion, but basic, repetitive, or missing deeper explanations.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Superficial language advice, poorly structured lessons, or content with minimal educational value.
- **0-2: Noise.** Off-topic content, spam, uninformative fragments, or promo material without educational substance.

# Evaluation guidance

Consider clarity, practical usefulness for learners, quality of vocabulary and grammar explanations, educational structure, and accessibility.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
